How pre-ordering works

Three steps to a full treat box

  1. Pick your treats

    Add anything from the weekly menu, or fill a mix-and-match box of six cookies.

  2. Choose a pickup slot

    Friday evening or Saturday morning. Cakes need five days' notice, everything else two.

  3. Collect and enjoy

    Show your order code at pickup and pay by card or cash. Boxes are packed the morning of.
