Good to know

When do I need to order by?

Thursday at 8 pm for that weekend's batch. Cakes need five days' notice, so order those by the Sunday or Monday before.

How do I pay?

Pay by card or cash when you pick up. Show your order code (it starts with OC-) and we'll hand over your box.

How long do the treats keep?

Cookies and brownies stay good for three days in an airtight tin. Cupcakes and cakes are best within two days, kept cool.

What about allergies?

Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs and tree nuts. GF items are made without gluten but in a shared kitchen, so add any allergies in your order notes.
